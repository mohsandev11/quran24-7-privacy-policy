1. Introduction

Welcome to Quran24-7 ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the Quran24-7 mobile application (the "App"), which provides Quranic content including reading, translations, and audio recitations.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

The App collects or lets its service providers collect the following:

  • Precise location – only if you allow it, so the App can calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The location is stored on your device and sent to the prayer-times service (Aladhan) to return times for your area. You can deny location permission and still use the rest of the App.
  • Advertising and device data – through Google AdMob, as described in Sections 3 and 4. This includes the Android Advertising ID, approximate location estimated from your IP address, interactions such as app opens and ad-related taps, and diagnostic information about how the App and ads perform.
  • On-device app data – reading progress, bookmarks, and settings stored locally on your device.

These services may collect anonymous, aggregated data on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy policies. The third-party services we may use include, but are not limited to:

  • Google AdMob – for displaying in-app advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve contextual or personalized ads. You may opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device's advertising settings.

  • Aladhan – to calculate prayer times. If you grant location permission, the App sends your coordinates to Aladhan and receives the prayer times for that location.

4. Advertising and Ad Identifiers

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may use advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ads. You can reset or limit use of these identifiers at any time:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

5. Children's Privacy

Quran24-7 is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

Location, reading progress, bookmarks, and settings stay on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Information sent to Aladhan is used to return prayer times for that request. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's privacy policy. You can email us to ask what information we hold and to request deletion of information we control.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your data, including under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and similar laws. You can:

  • Delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, as described in Section 4.
  • Email us to ask what information we hold and to request deletion of information we control.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.
